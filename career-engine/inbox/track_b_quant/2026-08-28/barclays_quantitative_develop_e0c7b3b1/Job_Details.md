## 📄 Original Job Description
Join Barclays as a Quantitative Developer in our Global Markets Linear Rates Quantitative Analytics team. In this role, you will drive the modernisation of our linear rates analytics infrastructure: maintaining and extending production analytics libraries, building automated reporting pipelines, and developing real-time streaming capabilities. The role blends quantitative development with a strong automation mindset, building robust frameworks and services that are used across the business.



To be successful as a Rates Quant Developer, you should have:

A master's degree or equivalent in Financial/Applied Mathematics, Physics, Engineering, or Computer ScienceExcellent Python and C++ programming skillsProven quantitative understanding of linear rates products and how they are priced and risk-managedOwnership mentality and ability to support multi-faceted initiatives end-to-endClear written and verbal communication for technical and non-technical stakeholders

Some other highly valued skills may include:

Knowledge of interest rate curve construction techniquesExperience with event-driven or stream-processing architectures (KDB+, Kafka etc.)Experience building automated workflows and data pipelines in a quantitative or trading environmentExperience working within large, complex C++ and Python codebases

You may be assessed on the key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen strategic thinking and digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.





This role is based in London.

 Purpose of the role

To provide quantitative and analytical expertise to support trading strategies, risk management, and decision-making within the investment banking domain, applying quantitative analysis, mathematical modelling, and technology to optimise trading and investment opportunities.
