## 📄 Original Job Description
A Software focused Project Manager will play a key role in the delivery of technical projects with significant software components across a range of industry sectors, including Communications, Industrial, Defence, Space and Security.
You’ll bring a strong Electronics related Degree, proven experience of managing complex software development projects (Embedded/DSP/Firmware/ML) and excellent people management skills.
A competitive salary will be offered with Hybrid working and a generous benefits package to include a Company Bonus, 10% Company Pension contribution, Private Healthcare, 25 days holiday, Life Assurance and Long Service awards.
Due to the nature of sensitive projects you must be eligible for UK Security Clearance.
The successful Software Project Manager will bring a good technical Degree, a strong background in 'deep tech', technology innovation and/or Embedded Software/Embedded Electronics Design and Development with a good understanding of modern software development practices and ideally bring experience of working in a consultancy style environment, with frequent client interaction.
Applications for this Software Project Manager are welcomed from self-motivated individuals who thrive on solving a range technical challenges, who are confident to drive performance and bring solid team leadership skills.
